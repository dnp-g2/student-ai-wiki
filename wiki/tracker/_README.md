# Tracker items appear here

Every assignment, quiz, exam and to-do gets one small page in this folder. The AI creates them when you say `add deadline ...` or ingest a course outline, and [[Dashboard]] shows them all.

You can edit any field in Obsidian's Properties panel and tick milestones as you finish them. Settings such as your timezone and term dates live in [[_config]]. Feel free to delete this readme.
